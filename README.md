Future Interns Power Bi Project_1
I have completed my first Data Analytics project with Future Interns. 

I performed data importing, data cleaning, and transformation to ensure data accuracy and consistency. I also created DAX measures to calculate key metrics such as total sales, profit, profit margin, total orders, and average discount.
This is an interactive Power Bi dashboard that tracks sales, profit, profit margin and average discount by time, state, region and product to support data driven decision making.

KPIS: Total orders, Total sales, Total customers, Total profit, Profit margin, Average discount

Insights
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the best selling product, generating $61.6k in sales and 25.2k in profit
- November is the highest performing month ($349.12k) while February is the least performing month ($60.17k). 
- The Western region brings the most revenue ($725.46k) 
- Technology is the top earning category ($ 836.15k)

Business recommendation
Management can review discount rates and shipping costs during during low-performing months and underperforming products to improve profitability
